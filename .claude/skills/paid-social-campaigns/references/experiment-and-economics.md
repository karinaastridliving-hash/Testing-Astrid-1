# Paid social experiment and economics
Contribution margin per order = revenue - variable costs - discounts - payment/fulfillment costs (adjust to client's accounting definitions).
Break-even CPA <= first-order contribution margin if optimizing for first-order profitability; lifetime value assumptions require actual retention evidence.
Break-even ROAS = revenue / allowable acquisition cost when both terms use consistent definitions; show assumptions.
Experiment table: hypothesis | independent variable | control | variant | event | audience | budget | duration | guardrails | stop criterion | result | confidence | next action.
Do not call a result statistically significant without a suitable test, valid sample and method. Platform-reported attribution is not necessarily incremental lift.
Governance: privacy/consent; discrimination-sensitive targeting restrictions; regulated industries; disclosures; rights; account access; human sign-off before spend. Never imply a campaign was launched.
