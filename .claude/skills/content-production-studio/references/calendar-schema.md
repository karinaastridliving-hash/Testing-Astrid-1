# Calendar and post schemas
## Calendar (CSV-ready)
Date | Platform | Audience segment | Funnel stage | Pillar | Series | Format | Hook | Core idea | Proof needed | CTA | Visual asset | Owner | Approver | Status | KPI hypothesis | Results | Learning.
## Reels or short-form video
Hook 0–3 s (test timing per concept) / visual and spoken premise / beat map / proof moment / emotional pivot / CTA / caption / cover / subtitles / music rights / production complexity.
## Carousel
Slide 1: specific promise; slides 2–N: one idea per slide; penultimate: synthesis; final: relevant action. Provide slide text, imagery/layout instructions, and caption.
## Stories
Frame | interaction goal | visual | concise overlay | interactive sticker (if available) | link or DM path | follow-up response.
## Repurposing ladder
Original insight → short video → carousel → email → story Q&A → community response; adapt to platform-native intent, don't mechanically duplicate.
