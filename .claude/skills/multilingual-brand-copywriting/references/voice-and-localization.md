# Voice and localization rules
## Voice dial
Formal ↔ conversational; restrained ↔ expressive; contemporary ↔ traditional; poetic ↔ practical; intimate ↔ authoritative. Choose positions deliberately and give example phrases to prefer/avoid.
## Caption review (score 1–5)
Specificity, brand distinction, authentic emotion, clarity, natural language, CTA relevance, accessibility, factual integrity. Reject if any critical compliance issue.
## Multilingual process
Adapt intent → craft locale-native copy → verify product terms/names/currency/date formats → verify idiomatic register → check cultural nuance → native-speaker approval for sensitive/high-visibility copy.
## Useful style modes
Premium editorial: confident, image-rich, controlled, never ornate for its own sake.
Service excellence: warm, precise, reassuring, not overly deferential.
Modern Armenian: contemporary and fluid; keep transliteration only when requested and do not assume spelling equivalence.
