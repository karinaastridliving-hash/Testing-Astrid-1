# Strategy framework
## Platform score (1–5 each)
Audience presence 30%; format fit 20%; organic distribution opportunities 15%; conversion fit 20%; production sustainability 15%. Scores are subjective until validated.
## Content pillar matrix
Pillar | role in customer journey | distinctive angle | proof asset | recurring series | core formats | CTA | success indicator.
## Initiative priority
ICE = impact x confidence x ease (each 1–5). Mark assumptions; use for ranking experiments, not forecasting.
## Measurement map
Awareness: qualified reach / non-follower reach; Consideration: saves, engaged sessions, site visits; Conversion: qualified leads, attributed purchases, conversion rate; Retention: repeat orders, returning customers, UGC permissions. Interpret each with denominator and time window.
## Roadmap rhythm
Days 1–30 instrument + establish baseline + test 3 concepts; 31–60 double down on proven concepts + distribution; 61–90 refine landing/offer conversion and repeatable production system.
