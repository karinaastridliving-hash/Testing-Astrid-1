# Community playbook
## Response severity
Routine (product/general): answer with brand-safe facts. Order/account issue: move to approved secure support channel without requesting sensitive information publicly. Safety/legal/discrimination threats: stop improvising; escalate promptly to designated owner and preserve records under policy.
## Creator assessment (1–5)
Audience relevance 30%, quality and authenticity 20%, content craft 20%, brand fit 15%, execution reliability 15%. Verify rates and engagement only with dated evidence.
## Outreach skeleton
Personal and specific observation → why audiences align → concise value proposition → scope and expected deliverables → compensation/rights/disclosure discussion → simple permission-based next step.
## Partnership terms checklist
Usage rights; paid amplification rights; term; territories; approval; deliverable dates; revision limits; payment; cancellation; disclosure; reporting; exclusivity.
## Experiment example
Hypothesis: creator demos create more qualified site sessions than broad giveaways. Primary metric: qualified sessions per 1,000 impressions. Baseline and tracking required; no invented benchmarks.
