# Research protocol and matrix
## Evidence grades
A = brand-owned or primary platform analytics; B = direct observation / official public sources; C = credible secondary reporting; D = hypothesis. Mark date, source, scope, and uncertainty. Avoid causal claims from correlation.
## Competitor matrix columns
Brand | segment | region | observable promise | proof | signature visual devices | content pillars | channel | observable CTA | unserved need | source/date | confidence.
## Audience card
Segment name; trigger; need; anxieties; desired transformation; value sensitivity; channels; buying objections; tone; likely content saves/shares; evidence and testing plan.
## Positioning formula
For [primary audience] who [job], [brand] is the [category/frame] that [specific benefit] because [reasons to believe]. Different from [alternatives], it [credible distinction].
## Red flags
Do not infer age, income, or psychology as fact from a photograph. Do not copy competitor posts. No fake competitor performance figures.
