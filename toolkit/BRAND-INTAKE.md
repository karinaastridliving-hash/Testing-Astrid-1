# Brand intake form
## Identity
Brand and contact; geography; products/offers; pricing range; category; brand story; target launches; brand website and owned channels; approved logos, typography, colors, and imagery.
## Commercial goals
Primary 90-day outcome; business conversion (DM, inquiry, checkout, reservation); lead time; fulfillment capacity; margin economics; seasonality; budget; best-performing offer; constraints.
## Audience
Existing buyers; buyer jobs; proof of audience needs; languages; objections; purchase triggers; customer FAQ; inclusion/accessibility needs.
## Social footprint
Current platforms and account links; last 90 days of owned analytics; current content archive; creative assets; top/weak content; approved testimonials; team and time capacity.
## Creative direction
Three admired reference brands with why; three brands/styles to avoid; desired emotional response; words to use/avoid; degree of editorial vs conversational tone.
## Production and governance
Who shoots, designs, edits, translates, approves, posts, responds? Lead times; IP and talent rights; social policies; customer privacy; ad spend authorization; escalation contact.
## Mandatory distinction
Mark answers as confirmed / assumed / unknown. Do not invent missing data.
