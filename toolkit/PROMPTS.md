# Copy-and-paste prompts
## 0 — Onboard a brand
Use the Brand & Market Intelligence and Social Media Strategy Architect Skills. Treat the following intake as the only confirmed facts. Build an assumptions register, ask for at most five high-impact missing inputs, and propose a lean 90-day social strategy with measurable hypotheses. [Paste BRAND-INTAKE answers]

## 1 — Brand diagnosis
Act as a senior brand strategy team. Analyze this brand positioning and three named competitors. Separate observed evidence from assumptions. Produce a competitor matrix, 3 audience segments, 2 distinctive positioning territories and a recommended messaging hierarchy. [Paste brand and evidence]

## 2 — 30-day content plan
Using our approved strategy, create a 30-day Instagram calendar with content pillars, a realistic posting cadence based on our resources, Reels, carousels and Stories, and status/owner/CTA/KPI columns. Fully develop week one as production-ready creative briefs. Do not invent live trends. [Paste strategy + resource constraints]

## 3 — Creative direction
Create 3 differentiated art-direction territories for our launch: lighting, settings, materials, compositions, typography, emotion, visual do/don't, and 6 specific social shot concepts. Select one based on brand strategy and production feasibility. [Paste brand brief]

## 4 — Premium multilingual captions
Write 3 campaign caption directions in English, Armenian and Russian for [specific product/event]. The tone is modern, tasteful, emotionally compelling and never clichéd. Localize by intent rather than translating literally; provide a short review note for each language. Do not make up product details.

## 5 — Partnership program
Design a 6-week creator collaboration pilot for [brand/market/budget]. Include creator screening rubric, rights/disclosure checklist, authentic outreach, deliverables, measurable hypotheses and stop/scale criteria.

## 6 — Paid campaign plan
Given this actual budget, offer economics and analytics setup, plan a paid social experiment with 6 creative variants. Mark all platform-specific targeting/policy details that require current verification. Do not recommend spend before attribution and consent checks. [Paste details]

## 7 — Monthly audit
Analyze these actual exported platform metrics. Recalculate rates with explicit denominators, compare equal date windows, split paid vs organic, distinguish facts from explanations, and deliver a concise performance report plus 5 prioritized experiments. [Attach data]

## Handoff rule
After each major deliverable, produce a short **handoff brief**: approved brand facts, decisions, unresolved questions, files needed, and next Skill to activate. Use this handoff in subsequent requests to avoid drift.
