# SMM Agency Toolkit — 8 specialist Skills
Version: 1.0 | November 2026 | Working language: English; adaptable to Armenian, Russian and French.

## What this is
Eight independent, reusable Skill packages for brand research, strategy, art direction, content production, copywriting/localization, community and creator relations, paid advertising, and analytics. Each package has its own valid `SKILL.md` with YAML `name` and `description`, plus a linked supporting reference file. The system is suitable for different brands, not tied to one business.

## Installation in Claude.ai
1. Download and unzip the complete `smm-agency-toolkit-complete.zip` **for storage or editing**, not as a single uploadable Skill.
2. Find the eight individual ZIP files supplied alongside this archive. Upload **each ZIP separately** from Claude's Skills interface (in current versions, look under Settings / Customize / Skills or the Add/Create Skill control). Each ZIP contains one Skill folder and its `SKILL.md`.
3. If Claude asks to enable code execution / file creation for custom Skills, follow the on-screen setup for your plan.
4. Start a conversation with the onboarding prompt in `PROMPTS.md` and provide approved brand material. Don't share passwords or private platform access credentials.
5. Test a specific request from `QA-TESTS.md`. Confirm Claude invokes the correct Skill; adjust the name/description if not.

Note: UI names and account eligibility may vary by plan and product version. See https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12512180-use-skills-in-claude

## Choose the Skill
- Who are we and who buys? → Brand & Market Intelligence
- Where should we invest and why? → Social Media Strategy Architect
- What should content look and feel like? → Visual Creative Direction
- What exactly are we publishing? → Content Production Studio
- How should it sound in each language? → Multilingual Brand Copywriting
- How do we build relationships? → Community Growth & Partnerships
- How do we spend paid budget? → Paid Social Campaigns
- What worked and what next? → Social Performance & Reporting

## Suggested sequence
**Discovery:** intelligence → strategy → creative direction.
**Monthly cycle:** strategy → content plan → creative + copy → approval → publish externally → analytics → iteration.
**Optional branches:** community/partnerships and paid social use the same brand strategy and voice constraints.

## Human checkpoints
Before launch approve: brand claims, pricing, visuals and rights, translations, public replies, creator terms, spend, access/privacy, scheduled dates. Skills do not connect to social platforms or publish without separately enabled and authorized integration.

## Included shared assets
- `BRAND-INTAKE.md`: complete intake questionnaire
- `PROMPTS.md`: immediate-use prompts and workflow handoffs
- `QA-TESTS.md`: test cases and scoring
- `templates/social-content-calendar.csv`: CSV planning sheet
- `templates/measurement-scorecard.csv`: KPI tracking structure
- `templates/creative-brief.md`: reusable creative brief
- `templates/monthly-report.md`: executive reporting template
- `templates/competitor-audit.csv`: research matrix
- `templates/approval-checklist.md`: pre-publish approval gate

## Updating
Copy versioned Skill folders before editing. Prefer changing a reference file or process step over continually expanding SKILL.md. Retest the original example prompts after every significant revision.
